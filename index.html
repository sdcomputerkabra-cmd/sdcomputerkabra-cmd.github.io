V380 Recorder

A personal, non-commercial home camera recorder.

V380 Recorder is a small private app (Android phone + Windows PC) that records the owner's own home security camera and keeps a backup copy in the owner's own Google account:

  • Recent clips are copied to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Longer recordings are uploaded as private videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

The app is used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
