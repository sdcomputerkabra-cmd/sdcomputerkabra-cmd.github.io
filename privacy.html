Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

V380 Recorder is a personal app used only by its owner to record the owner's own home camera.

What data the app uses

  • Camera video from the owner's camera on the owner's home network.
  • Google Drive (scope drive.file): the app only creates and manages the video files it uploads itself. It cannot see any other files in the Drive.
  • YouTube: the app uploads recordings as private videos to the owner's own channel and deletes them after the retention period chosen by the owner.

Where data goes

Video stays on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own Google account. The app has no server of its own, does not collect analytics, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone. Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own devices.

Google API data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
